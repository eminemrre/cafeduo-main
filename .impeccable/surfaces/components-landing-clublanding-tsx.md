# Surface brief: public landing (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: students in a partner café, on a phone, often from a table QR code. Action: "Masaya katıl" (register / go to panel); "Giriş yap" always reachable. Proof: the real games, rules and reward catalogue in PRODUCT.md; no invented numbers, cafés or people. Constraints: DESIGN.md "Masa Oyuncakları" (Vitrin register) is binding; works without WebGL, with reduced motion and keyboard; routes, auth modal and lazyWithRetry unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The café table is a toy set and the opponent is the next table. Refuses the category default of a headline over three feature cards and a phone mockup.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched candy fields per section (limon hero, ice games, lokum rewards, gök steps), ink text only, Bagel Fat One headlines, Golos Text body, pill buttons, matte vinyl 3D toys (gök knight, lokum cup, fıstık coin, white MASA07 tent, limon question card, lokum target) with one soft contact shadow; no UI shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees the toys land on the table, taps the knight, watches it make a knight's move toward MASA07, sees the coin flip to +20, understands "a short game against the next table pays in café rewards", then taps "Masaya katıl".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Limon field. Left: "Rakibin yan masada." in Bagel Fat One (~120 px desktop, ~56 px phone), one sentence, ink pill "Masaya katıl", text button "Giriş yap". Right (below on phones): the live toy scene, toys large and cropped by the right edge, hint "Ata dokun, ilk hamleni yap". Sticky white header with both actions.

FORM: user-pinned direction from references A (game scene) and B (toy, candy); position 1 of 1. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
